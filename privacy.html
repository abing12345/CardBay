一句话：卡备不收集、不上传你的任何个人信息，也不上传你的照片。

In one sentence: CardBay collects nothing, uploads nothing, and never sends your photos anywhere.

所有导入都在你自己的 Mac 上、在你指定的磁盘之间完成。App 本身不发起任何网络请求。

Every import happens on your own Mac, between the disks you choose. The app itself makes no network requests.

1. 适用范围

本政策适用于 macOS 应用 CardBay（中文名：卡备），包括你从 Mac App Store 下载的版本以及它的后续更新。

2. 我们收集哪些信息

什么都不收集。具体地说：

  • 没有账号体系，不需要注册，不需要提供姓名、邮箱或手机号；
  • 没有统计分析、没有崩溃上报、没有广告 SDK；
  • 不使用任何跨 App 追踪，也不建立任何用于广告或画像的标识符；
  • 不读取你的通讯录、日历、位置、麦克风、相机或任何与导入无关的数据。

3. 你的数据保存在哪里

卡备运行时产生的所有数据都留在你自己的 Mac 上：

数据存放位置是否外发
照片与视频文件 只从你授权的存储卡复制到你选择的目标文件夹 否
界面偏好（语言、排序方式、上次的选择等） 本机 App 容器内的偏好设置 否
授权记录（安全作用域书签） 本机 App 容器内，用于下次免重复授权访问同一张卡或文件夹 否
购买状态（是否已解锁完整版） 本机记录，来源于 App Store 的交易信息 否
运行日志 本机 App 容器内，仅用于你自己排查问题 否

App 容器位于 ~/Library/Containers/com.abing.cardbay.mas，你可以随时查看或删除。

4. 网络访问与内购

  • App 本身不发起网络请求，也不包含任何第三方联网组件。
  • 唯一涉及网络的动作是购买或恢复购买：它由 App Store 的系统内购流程完成。
  • 支付信息（支付方式、账单姓名、地址等）由 Apple 处理，我们不接触、也无法获取。卡备只会从系统得知「这台设备当前是否已购买」，用来决定是否解锁完整功能。
  • Apple 对相关数据的处理适用 Apple 隐私政策。

5. 权限与沙盒

卡备在 macOS 的沙盒（App Sandbox）中运行，只在你明确授权的范围内访问文件。它会请求的权限只有三类，每一项都只服务于一个具体功能：

  • 可移除卷（存储卡） —— 读取卡里的照片；
  • 桌面文件夹 —— 在桌面放置一个快捷方式；
  • 文稿文件夹 —— 把照片导入到你选择的文件夹。

你可以在「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」里随时收回这些授权；收回后卡备将无法读取相应位置，但不会影响你已经导入到磁盘上的文件。

6. 第三方

卡备不含任何第三方分析、广告、追踪或社交 SDK。我们不向任何第三方出售、出租或共享你的数据 —— 因为我们根本没有接触到这些数据。

7. 数据的保留与删除

  • 卸载卡备（把 App 拖进废纸篓）即可停止一切本地处理。
  • 如需一并清除本机设置与记录，删除容器目录 ~/Library/Containers/com.abing.cardbay.mas 即可。
  • 你在 App Store 的购买记录由 Apple 保留和管理，不受卸载影响，也无法由我们代为删除。

8. 儿童隐私

卡备不是面向儿童的产品，也不会收集任何用户（包括儿童）的个人信息。

9. 关于你的隐私权利

由于卡备不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人信息，因此不存在需要我们提供、更正或删除的个人数据 —— 包括在 GDPR、CCPA 等法律框架下通常需要响应的那类请求。你对自己机器上的文件拥有完全控制权。

10. 本政策的变更

如果本政策有实质变更，我们会更新本页面并修改顶部的生效日期。建议你在购买新版本或遇到疑问时回看本页。

11. 联系我们

对本政策或数据处理的任何疑问，欢迎写信到 imeaningwhy@163.com。

1. Scope

This policy applies to the macOS application CardBay, including the version you download from the Mac App Store and all subsequent updates.

2. What we collect

Nothing. Specifically:

  • No accounts. No sign-up, no name, no email address, no phone number.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs.
  • No cross-app tracking, and no identifiers created for advertising or profiling.
  • No access to your contacts, calendar, location, microphone, camera, or anything unrelated to importing photos.

3. Where your data lives

Everything CardBay produces stays on your own Mac:

DataWhere it isSent out?
Photo and video files Copied only from the card you authorise to the destination folder you choose No
Interface preferences (language, sorting, last selection) Preferences inside the app’s own container on your Mac No
Authorisation records (security-scoped bookmarks) Local app container, so the same card or folder need not be authorised again No
Purchase status (whether the full version is unlocked) A local record derived from App Store transaction information No
Runtime logs Inside the local app container, for your own troubleshooting No

The container lives at ~/Library/Containers/com.abing.cardbay.mas. You can inspect or delete it at any time.

4. Network access and purchases

  • The app makes no network requests and bundles no third-party networking components.
  • The only network-related action is buying or restoring a purchase, handled by the App Store in-app purchase flow.
  • Payment details (method, billing name, address) are processed by Apple. We never see them. The app only learns whether this device currently owns the purchase, in order to unlock the full feature set.
  • Apple’s handling of that data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions and the sandbox

CardBay runs inside the macOS App Sandbox and only accesses files within the scope you explicitly authorise. It asks for three permissions, each serving one concrete feature:

  • Removable volumes (camera cards) — to read the photos on your card;
  • Desktop folder — to place a shortcut on your Desktop;
  • Documents folder — to import photos into the folder you choose.

You can revoke any of these at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Revoking them stops CardBay from reading that location, but does not affect files already imported to your disks.

6. Third parties

CardBay contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or social SDKs. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone — we never have access to it in the first place.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Uninstalling CardBay (dragging it to the Trash) stops all local processing.
  • To also clear local settings and records, delete the container folder ~/Library/Containers/com.abing.cardbay.mas.
  • Your App Store purchase record is held by Apple, survives uninstallation, and cannot be deleted by us.

8. Children’s privacy

CardBay is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Your privacy rights

Because CardBay does not collect, store or transmit personal information, there is no personal data for us to disclose, correct or delete — including requests that would ordinarily be answered under frameworks such as the GDPR or CCPA. You retain full control of the files on your own machine.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. If in doubt, come back and check.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or about data handling? Write to imeaningwhy@163.com.
