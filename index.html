插卡 → 勾选 → 导入。卡备把摄影师倒卡的那十几步，压成两三下。

Insert card → tick → import. CardBay turns the ten-odd steps of offloading a card into two or three.

摄影师做的 macOS 倒卡工具：插上卡自动弹窗，照片按相机品牌和格式自动分好栏，点一下「新拍」只挑出这次拍的，再点一下就进你常存的文件夹。

A card offloading tool made by a photographer, for macOS. The window opens by itself when you insert a card; photos are sorted into columns by camera brand and file format; one click on “New” picks only what you shot today; another click sends them to the folder you always use.

主要功能What it does

  • 插卡自动弹出 —— 插上卡，窗口自己出来。不用找图标，不用先打开软件。Opens on insert — plug in a card and the window appears. No hunting for an icon, no launching the app first.
  • 按「相机品牌 + 格式」自动分栏 —— 卡里有什么就分什么栏，RAW 和 JPG 各占一栏。打开就是排好的，不用自己进 DCIM 翻文件夹。Columns by brand & format — RAW and JPG each get their own column, brands stay apart. Everything is already laid out; no digging through DCIM.
  • 「新拍」一键勾选 —— 卡里常混着这次拍的、上次没导完的、更早的旧片。点一下「新拍」，只勾出这一次新拍的。One-click “New” — cards often hold today’s shots plus leftovers from last time. One click ticks only what is new since your last import.
  • 每栏独立排序 —— 按名称或按时间排，同一个按钮再点一下换方向（正序 ⇄ 倒序）。Per-column sorting — sort by name or time; click the same button again to flip the direction.
  • 大图预览 —— 鼠标停在文件上，光标旁就是大图。挑片、排片不用一张张点开看。Hover preview — a large preview follows the cursor. Cull and sequence without opening files one by one.
  • 目标文件夹常驻 —— 常用文件夹存成一张张小卡片，可拖动排序、可从访达直接拖进来。导图时点一下就好。Destinations always at hand — your usual folders sit as cards on the right; reorder them or drag folders in from Finder.
  • 同名绝不覆盖 —— 目标里已有同名文件，自动加尾号另存一份，绝不会盖掉你已有的原片。Never overwrites — if a file with the same name exists, a numbered copy is saved alongside it. Your originals stay untouched.
  • 导过的不重复导 —— 换一个目标文件夹，之前导过的不会再倒一遍。No duplicate imports — change the destination and previously imported files are still recognised, not copied again.
  • 一次插多张卡，只开一个窗口 —— 多卡一起处理，按卡分组，不用轮流插、轮流导。Several cards, one window — plug in multiple cards and they are grouped inside a single window.
  • 顺手的清理 —— 挑出不要的，直接移进废纸篓（会先问你一次，废纸篓里也能找回）。Easy cleanup — move unwanted files to the Trash after a confirmation; they remain recoverable from the Trash.

常见问题Frequently asked questions

插上卡没有自动弹出窗口？The window did not open when I inserted a card.

按顺序检查三件事：① 卡备是否正在运行（如果刚开机，等几秒让后台服务就位）；② 系统设置 → 通用 → 登录项，确认卡备的后台项已被允许；③ 卡是否已经在访达里出现 —— 如果系统本身没挂载这张卡，卡备也读不到。

Check three things in order: (1) is CardBay running (after a fresh login, give the background service a moment); (2) System Settings → General → Login Items — make sure CardBay’s background item is allowed; (3) does the card show up in Finder at all? If macOS has not mounted it, CardBay cannot see it either.

也可以直接打开卡备，窗口里点「刷新」重新扫描一次。

You can also just open CardBay and rescan from the window.

提示「需要授权才能读取存储卡」怎么办？It says “Card Access Needed”. What do I do?

这是 macOS 沙盒的授权提示。点提示里的按钮，在弹出的面板里选中存储卡（或选中整个「位置」一栏），然后按「打开」。授权一次之后，同一张卡下次插上就不用再点了。

This is the macOS sandbox asking for permission. Click the button in the prompt, select your card (or the whole “Locations” section) in the panel, and confirm. Once granted, the same card will not ask again.

卡备只读取你亲自授权过的存储卡与文件夹，不会碰其它位置。

CardBay only reads the cards and folders you have explicitly authorised — nothing else.

换了一台电脑，怎么找回已购买？I am on a new Mac. How do I get my purchase back?

用同一 Apple ID 登录后打开卡备，在购买窗口点「恢复购买」即可，不需要重新付费。

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open CardBay, and click “Restore Purchases” in the purchase window. No need to pay again.

会覆盖我硬盘上的同名文件吗？Will it overwrite files that already exist?

不会。目标文件夹里已有同名文件时，卡备会自动加尾号另存，你的原片一个都不会被动。

No. If a file with the same name already exists, CardBay saves a numbered copy instead. Your existing files are never modified.

删掉卡里的照片，能恢复吗？I deleted photos from the card — can they be recovered?

卡备的删除是「移进废纸篓」，操作前会先确认一次。在废纸篓被清空之前，都可以从废纸篓里放回去。

Deleting in CardBay moves files to the Trash after a confirmation. Until the Trash is emptied, you can put them back.

照片会被上传到云端吗？Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

不会。所有照片只在你自己的磁盘之间复制，卡备不做任何上传。详见隐私政策。

No. Photos are only copied between your own disks. Nothing is uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

怎么切换界面语言？How do I change the interface language?

卡备内置中文与英文界面，在菜单里选择「语言」即可切换。

CardBay ships with Chinese and English interfaces. Pick your language from the “Language” item in the menu.

如何彻底卸载？How do I remove it completely?

把卡备从「应用程序」拖进废纸篓即可。如果你还想清掉本机留下的设置与记录，可以在「前往文件夹」里打开 ~/Library/Containers/com.abing.cardbay.mas 一并删除。卸载不影响你在 App Store 的购买记录。

Drag CardBay from Applications to the Trash. To also remove local settings and records, open ~/Library/Containers/com.abing.cardbay.mas via “Go to Folder” and delete it. Removing the app does not affect your App Store purchase.
